Product Details

Description

Add timeless charm to your dining space with the Oak & Lily Table Runner in Ivory & Beige Ikat Print. Crafted from premium 100% cotton with anti-skid and heat-resistant features, it’s designed to offer both function and grace. Whether it’s your dining table, coffee nook, or console, this runner enhances your space effortlessly while protecting it from spills and scratches.

Key Features

- Subtle Ikat design in ivory & beige tones
- 100% cotton with European-quality finish
- Anti-skid and heat-resistant
- Professionally hemmed edges
- Perfect for dining, coffee, or accent tables
