Product Details

Description

Refresh your centre table with timeless elegance using the Oak & Lily Table Cloth in a Blue Check Print. Made from premium 100% cotton and crafted to European quality standards, this washable cloth combines classic style with practical design. The anti-slip surface keeps it secure on coffee tables, while the 100 × 140 cm size makes it perfect for everyday use or special gatherings. Professionally hemmed and easy to care for, it's the perfect addition to any modern Indian living space.

Key Features

- Elegant blue check design for classic appeal
- 100% cotton, soft and breathable fabric
- Anti-slip construction for everyday practicality
- Fully washable and easy to maintain
- Ideal size for centre tables and coffee setups

Care Instructions

Machine wash cold, tumble dry low, warm iron. No bleach. Do not dry clean.
